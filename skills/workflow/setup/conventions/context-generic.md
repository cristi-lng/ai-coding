## Constraints

- Do not create a new abstraction, utility or pattern when an equivalent already exists. Search the codebase first.
- Do not introduce a second way to solve a problem the project already solves.
- Do not refactor, rename, or touch code that is unrelated to your task.
- Do not patch symptoms with workarounds or feature flags. Find and fix the root cause instead.
- Do not leave dead code, unused imports, or commented-out blocks behind.
- Do not catch errors broadly when you can catch them specifically, and never silently swallow them.
- Do not add a dependency unless it is genuinely necessary.
- Do not present work you have not fully thought through.
- If tests fail after your change, fix the underlying cause rather than editing the tests to pass.
